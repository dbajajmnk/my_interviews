## 1. Purpose

Convert the supplied 10-module Software Design & Architecture curriculum
into a compact, interview-focused preparation plan.

**Important:** The 12 packs do not expand or replace the supplied
curriculum. They reorganize its topics into interview-friendly
preparation units while preserving full curriculum coverage.

The objective is to prepare the candidate to:

-   Understand and explain architecture concepts clearly.
-   Connect concepts with practical engineering decisions.
-   Visualize and communicate architecture.
-   Compare alternatives and explain trade-offs.
-   Handle scenario-based questions.
-   Defend decisions through follow-up and cross-questions.
-   Demonstrate architecture thinking using the curriculum case studies.

# 3. Mandatory Interview Framework for Every Pack

Every pack must follow this structure:

1.  **Objective**
2.  **Simple Analogy**
3.  **Visualization**
4.  **Mind Map**
5.  **Core Concepts**
6.  **Engineering Depth**
7.  **Common Mistakes / Red Flags**
8.  **Examples / Practical Usage**
9.  **Practice / Scenario**
10. **Interview Questions**
11. **Follow-Up / Cross-Questions**
12. **Quick Revision Notes**
13. **References**
14. **Readiness Gate**

### Interview Controls

Every important answer must be checked for:

-   Evidence First
-   No bluffing / invented experience
-   Red-Flag Check
-   Follow-Up Survival
-   Explain-on-Demand
-   Scenario Defense
-   Trade-Off Defense
-   Alternative Approach
-   One pack at a time
-   Do not move forward until the readiness gate passes

Question categories are used only where they genuinely fit the topic.

# 4. Architecture Answer Framework

For architecture or system-design questions, use:

**Requirements → Assumptions → Functional Requirements → Non-functional
Requirements → Constraints → High-Level Architecture → Components → Data
→ APIs → Communication → Security → Scalability → Availability → Failure
Handling → Deployment → Trade-offs → Alternatives → Recommendation**

This is an interview answering framework, not an additional curriculum
topic list.
