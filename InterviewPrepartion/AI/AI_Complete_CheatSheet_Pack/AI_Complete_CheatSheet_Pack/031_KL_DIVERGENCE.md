# KL Divergence

## What / Why

Measures how one distribution differs from another.

## Mental Model

```text
KL(P||Q)
```

## When to Use

Use distillation, variational methods, RLHF-like objectives.

## Common Mistake / Interview Trap

KL is asymmetric.
