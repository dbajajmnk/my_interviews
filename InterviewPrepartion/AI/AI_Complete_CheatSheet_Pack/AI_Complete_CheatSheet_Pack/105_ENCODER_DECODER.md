# Encoder Decoder

## What / Why

Encoder builds representation; decoder generates output.

## Mental Model

```text
source → encoder → decoder → target
```

## When to Use

Use translation/seq2seq models.

## Common Mistake / Interview Trap

Decoder-only LLMs use different architecture.
