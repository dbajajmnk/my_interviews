1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why this Agent SDK role?
3. How do you mentor developers?
4. How do you drive coding standards?
5. Tell me about a difficult architecture decision.
6. How do you handle disagreement?
7. How do you balance speed and quality?
8. How do you decide build vs buy?
9. How do you handle production incidents?
10. How do you communicate technical risk?
11. How do you introduce a new platform?
12. How do you improve developer adoption?
13. How do you define engineering quality?
14. How do you deal with ambiguous requirements?
15. How do you prioritize tech debt?
16. How do you approach backward compatibility?
17. How do you review APIs/SDKs?
18. How do you make a team self-sufficient?
19. How do you measure platform success?
20. What would you do in your first 90 days?
